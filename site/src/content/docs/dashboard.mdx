Numbers below come from trimwire users who **opted in** with `trimwire share
stats`. Everything is **anonymous, content-free, and aggregated** — coarse
buckets only, with small groups suppressed (k-anonymity). No prompts, code,
paths, or identifiers are ever collected. See
[how the data is collected & protected](/guides/telemetry/).

<div class="tw-reading-help">

**Reading the table:** each row is a *cohort* — grouped by the user's Claude Code
model, profile, summarizer backend, and session length. The columns are the
always-on **model-free pruning** metrics (reduction, cache stability, strategy
fire rates). Expand a row (▶) for its per-strategy breakdown and, where the
optional summarizer was used, its stats. (The "model" is your Claude session
model, not the summarizer model.)
